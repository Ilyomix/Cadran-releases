# Everything Cadran Does.

Every feature, in detail, in the version you can download today. The list changes with each release.

Everything here is free, except what carries the Pro badge. Where Free and Pro appear together, the line says which part is which.

## On your desktop <!-- desktop -->

- **On the wallpaper.** The clock sits on your desktop, above the wallpaper and below your icons and every window.
- **Clicks pass through.** Click, drag and select on the desktop under the clock as if it were not there.
- **On every Space.** The clock stays in place on every Space.
- **On every display.** Each connected display gets its own clock, and the clocks follow when you plug or unplug a display.
- **Follows your wallpaper.** The clock reads your wallpaper again when you change it or switch Space, so Automatic Color stays legible.
- **Pause When Hidden.** Cadran stops redrawing while every clock is covered, for example by full-screen apps, or the display sleeps, and shows the right time as soon as it is back.
- **Burn-in Protection.** On Apple XDR and OLED displays, the clock shifts by up to 2 points every 5 minutes to spare the screen.
- **Show on All Displays.** Turn the clock on or off on every display. With Pro, on one display at a time too. `Free + Pro`
- **Time Format.** Auto, following macOS, 12h or 24h. Pura, Dessau and Méridien are 12-hour dials, Prose spells the time in words and Metric shows the day as a decimal.
- **Clock Language.** The language of dates, weekdays and word clocks: Auto or any of the 11 languages, whatever the language of the interface.
- **Show Seconds.** One switch for the seconds of every face and display. The screen saver keeps its own.
- **Animations.** Turn the clock’s animations off on every display, or on one display from the editor. Reduce Motion in macOS turns them off too.

## The clock faces <!-- faces -->

- **Pura.** Three hands, no dial, pure simplicity. Second Hand. A 12-hour dial.
- **Orbit.** Concentric arcs that fill as time passes. Seconds Ring. 12h or 24h.
- **Clima.** Live weather and time, side by side. Live weather, with Location access. Show Seconds. Two complications. 12h or 24h.
- **Departure.** Airport-style flip clock with rolling date. Blinking Separator. One complication on its date line. Its own flap color and material. 12h or 24h.
- **Titan.** Giant stacked numerals that fill the screen. No seconds. 12h or 24h.
- **Arcade.** Retro 5×7 pixel digits. Blinking Separator. 12h or 24h.
- **Prose.** A letter grid that spells the time in words. The time in words, in 10 languages. No seconds. `Pro`
- **Spectrum.** A color generated from the current time. Show Seconds. 12h or 24h. `Pro`
- **Sablier.** A dot-matrix hourglass that drains in real time. Blinking Separator. 12h or 24h. `Pro`
- **Cipher.** Hours, minutes, and seconds encoded in binary. Show Seconds. 12h or 24h. `Pro`
- **Adage.** Time in classic typography with a daily quote. A new quote and its author each day. Blinking Separator. 12h or 24h. `Pro`
- **Syntax.** Time, weather, and battery as a live code block. Time, weather and battery as code; the weather needs Location access. Show Seconds. 12h or 24h. `Pro`
- **Platform.** Left-aligned digits, station-board style. Blinking Separator. One complication on its date line. 12h or 24h. `Pro`
- **Phase.** Real-time moon phase with date and time. Blinking Separator. One complication on its date line. 12h or 24h. `Pro`
- **Foundry.** Rotating digits, mechanical counter style. Show Seconds. 12h or 24h. `Pro`
- **Quartz.** Retro seven-segment LCD display. Show Seconds. One complication on its date line. 12h or 24h. On the desktop only, not as a screen saver. `Pro`
- **Dessau.** Analog dial with animated graduations. Seconds Marker. A 12-hour dial. `Pro`
- **Metric.** The day as a decimal from 0 to 1,000. Decimal Precision. One complication in its date header. The day as a decimal, from 0 to 1,000. `Pro`
- **Horizon.** A sweep hand circling a ring each minute. Seconds Progress. One complication on its date line. 12h or 24h. `Pro`
- **Halftone.** Dot columns that fill as the minutes pass. No seconds. 12h or 24h. `Pro`
- **Régulateur.** Minutes at the center, hours and seconds in balance. Seconds Subdial. One complication. Its own colors for each hand and for the graduations. 12h or 24h. `Pro`
- **Folio.** Time and the whole month, on one page. Daily event counts and your next event, with Calendar access. Show Seconds. 12h or 24h. `Pro`
- **Méridien.** Roman numerals and an openwork hour hand. Second Hand. One complication in its date window. A 12-hour dial. `Pro`
- **Secteur.** Time in motion on vertical rulers. Seconds Ruler. Two complications, on its date line and its seconds ruler. 12h or 24h. `Pro`
- **Aperture.** Analog time, seen up close. Seconds Arc. One complication on its ruler. Its own colors for each hand. 12h or 24h. It fills the screen, so it does not move or resize. `Pro`

## Clock color <!-- color -->

- **Automatic Color.** Keeps the clock light or dark to suit the background. Over your wallpaper it takes the wallpaper’s dominant color, with a shadow that keeps it readable.
- **Color palette.** Black, Charcoal, Slate, Mist, Paper, Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Teal, Mint, Cyan, Blue, Indigo, Brown, Purple and Pink. `Pro`
- **Custom Color.** Any color, with a saturation and brightness square, a hue slider and a hex field. `Pro`
- **Recent Colors.** Your last 8 custom colors stay under the palette. `Pro`
- **A color for each face.** Each face remembers its clock color and background. With Pro, each display can have its own too. `Free + Pro`
- **Hand Colors.** On Régulateur and Aperture, color all the hands together, or the hour, minute and second hand one by one. `Pro`
- **Graduations.** Gives Régulateur’s dial markings their own color, or lets them follow the clock color. `Pro`
- **Flap Color.** Departure’s flaps in a palette or custom color. Automatic matches them to the background. `Pro`
- **Flap material.** Classic, Frosted Glass or Liquid Glass flaps on Departure. Liquid Glass needs macOS 26 and is the default over a Transparent background.
- **Liquid Glass.** On macOS 27, the clock drawn in glass over your wallpaper, like the clock on the Lock Screen: on Titan, Clima, Platform, Adage, Phase, Departure, Halftone, Folio and Prose. `Pro`
- **Color under Liquid Glass.** While Liquid Glass is on, the clock color, graduations and hand colors rest, because the glass supplies the look. `Pro`
- **Reset Clock Colors.** Puts back the clock color, material, graduations and hand colors of the face you are editing.
- **Apply to All Faces…** Copies the background and clock color of the face you are editing to every other face, after a confirmation, in one Undo step. With Pro, on this display or on all of them. `Free + Pro`

## Backgrounds <!-- backgrounds -->

- **Transparent.** Your own wallpaper stays behind the clock. It is the default for a new install.
- **Solid colors.** Free: Black, Midnight, Charcoal, Slate, Paper, Blue, Purple, Red, Green, and Teal. With Pro: Mist, Indigo, Pink, Orange, Yellow, Mint, Cyan, and Brown. `Free + Pro`
- **Gradients.** Free: Graphite, Night, Blue, Mint, Forest, Sunset, Coral, Rose, Candy, and Ocean. With Pro: Cyan, Aqua, Lime, Sunrise, Magenta, Iris, Pearl, and Berry. `Free + Pro`
- **Dynamic Sky.** A sky that changes with the time of day, following the sun where you are when Cadran knows your location. `Pro`
- **Custom solid color.** Any solid color, with the same picker as the clock. `Pro`
- **Custom gradient.** Your own gradient, from a start color, an end color and an angle. `Pro`
- **Color stops.** Click the gradient bar to add a stop, drag it to move it or away to remove it, and recolor each one. `Pro`
- **Gradient angle.** An angle dial, or type the degrees. `Pro`
- **Swap Colors.** Swaps the start and end colors, and mirrors the stops. `Pro`
- **Recent colors and gradients.** Your last 8 custom colors and last 8 custom gradients, with their stops and angle, stay in the palette. `Pro`
- **Reset Background.** Returns the face’s background to Transparent.

## Custom message <!-- message -->

- **Message.** A personal note on the desktop beside your clock, in the clock’s ink and font: up to 200 characters on up to 3 lines.
- **A message for each face.** Each face keeps its own message, on all its displays. Empty the field to hide it.
- **Move the message.** Drag it anywhere on the screen, apart from the clock.
- **Resize the message.** Drag a corner, or use the keyboard, from 30% to 600%.
- **Automatic placement.** Until you move it, the message sits near the clock and keeps clear of it, even at the clock’s widest.
- **Reset Message Layout.** Puts the message back in its automatic place and size.

## Move and resize <!-- move -->

- **Move & Resize.** Drag the clock anywhere on the screen and resize it live, on every face except Aperture. `Pro`
- **Resize handles.** Eight handles, on the edges and the corners, each with its own pointer. An open hand over the clock, a closed hand while you drag. `Pro`
- **Size range.** From 30% to 100% of its default size. The clock cannot leave the screen. `Pro`
- **Snap to the default place.** Near its default place, an outline shows it, the clock snaps to it, and a trackpad with haptics taps. `Pro`
- **Saved for each face and display.** Each face keeps its own place and size on each display. `Pro`
- **Reset.** Returns the clock to its default place and size. `Pro`

- **A different face on each display.** Without Pro, every display shows the same face. `Pro`
- **Color, background and complications per display.** With Pro, each display can have its own clock color, background, complications and font. Without it, an edit reaches every display. `Pro`
- **Apply to.** In the gallery, choose All displays. With Pro, or one display. `Free + Pro`

## Screen saver <!-- screensaver -->

- **Cadran as your screen saver.** Your clock full screen as a macOS screen saver: any face except Quartz, 24 in all, Pro faces included.
- **Same as Desktop.** Each display’s screen saver shows that display’s clock: its face, font, colors, message and background. On by default.
- **Its own settings.** Turn Same as Desktop off to give the screen saver its own face, background, message, font and color, on a full-size page with the desktop’s editing bar.
- **Clock Face.** Choose the screen saver’s face in a searchable list with previews.
- **Time format and seconds.** With its own settings, the screen saver has its own Time Format and its own seconds control.
- **Power Saving Mode.** Updates the clock less often to use less energy: faces that can, redraw once a minute while the seconds are hidden.
- **Clock Color.** With its own settings, Automatic Color, or a palette or custom color with Pro. `Free + Pro`
- **Desktop Wallpaper.** A still of your wallpaper behind the clock. If it cannot load, the screen saver’s own background shows, with a note.
- **Backgrounds in the screen saver.** Dynamic Sky and all 36 solid colors and gradients are free here. Gradients drift slowly.
- **Fluid Gradient.** A switch that sets a gradient in motion like a liquid. Reduce Motion keeps it still.
- **Its own font.** With its own settings, the screen saver has its own Font menu, on the faces that offer one.
- **A message.** Under the clock, its own or, with Same as Desktop, the desktop’s.
- **Its own place and size.** With its own settings, move and resize its clock on the page. Centered by default. `Pro`
- **Install, Reinstall, Uninstall.** From the Screen Saver page, in one click, without changing your screen saver settings. Uninstall asks first.
- **Status.** A capsule says Not installed, Not used as Screen Saver, Used as Screen Saver or Installed.
- **Open in System Settings…** Opens System Settings to choose Cadran and its start time, with the steps if it cannot be selected for you.
- **Use as Screen Saver.** On a face’s card, on its page or in the Gallery menu: sets it as the screen saver and installs it if needed.
- **Live preview.** The Screen Saver page shows the screen saver with your face, background and message.
- **Weather, Playing Now and your wallpaper.** With Same as Desktop on, while Cadran is open, the screen saver shows the desktop’s weather, the track playing and your wallpaper, even one kept in Downloads, Desktop or Documents. `since 2.0.3`
- **Calendar stays on the desktop.** Your events never show in the screen saver.
- **Two clocks on the Lock Screen.** On macOS 26 and later, a hint says how to turn off the large clock of the Lock Screen.
- **Liquid Glass in the screen saver.** On macOS 27, a Liquid Glass clock keeps its glass when Same as Desktop is on and the desktop is Transparent. `since 2.0.2` `Pro`
- **Options in System Settings.** The Options button of System Settings opens the screen saver’s own settings, background and message.

## Weather, music and calendar <!-- data -->

- **Live weather.** Temperature, wind, conditions, sunrise and sunset where you are, refreshed about every 30 minutes, on Clima, Syntax and the weather complications. `Free + Pro`
- **Temperature and Wind Speed.** °C or °F, km/h or mph, on every face and display.
- **Location.** Asked only for local weather and the sun, at city-level accuracy, from the face’s card, the editor or Settings.
- **Playing Now.** The track playing in Music, Spotify or any app that reports what it plays. `Pro`
- **Calendar.** Read only, when you choose Connect Calendar. Cadran never creates, edits or deletes an event.
- **Daily event counts.** Folio shows how many events each day of the month has, from all your calendars. `Pro`
- **Next event on Folio.** Its title and start, within 30 days; canceled and declined events are skipped. `Pro`
- **Events stay private.** Event details never show in gallery previews or the screen saver.

## The desktop editor <!-- editor -->

- **Customize.** Edit the clock right on your wallpaper, from a bar that names the face and the display.

## Install, updates and help <!-- help -->

- **Direct download.** A disk image from cadranapp.com, or Homebrew with brew install --cask cadran.
- **In-app updates.** Cadran checks for a new version at launch and installs it in a few clicks.
- **Check for Updates…** Checks now and says Up to date or Version X is available.
- **Welcome and Guided Tour.** On first launch, a tour sets up your clock and your screen saver. Skip it, and replay it from the Help menu.
- **What’s New.** After an update, the version’s notes open once; the full history stays one click away.
- **Cadran Support, Visit Website, Report a Bug…** Open the site in your language. Nothing from the app is attached.
- **About Cadran.** The version and build, the links to the site, privacy and terms, and the credits.
- **Startup check.** If Cadran’s integrity check fails at launch, an alert offers Continue or Open Website.

## Privacy <!-- privacy -->

- **No account, no tracking.** No account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reports in the app.
- **It stays on your Mac.** Your preferences, wallpaper colors and license file stay on the Mac. Wallpapers, calendars and music never leave it.
- **Weather.** If you use it, your approximate location goes to Apple’s weather service.
- **License.** Activating and checking Pro sends the key and your Mac’s name to the license service. `Pro`
- **Updates.** Checking for and downloading new versions, from GitHub.
- **Asked only when needed.** Location, Calendar and a wallpaper kept in Desktop, Documents, Downloads, iCloud Drive or on another volume are asked for when a face or a setting needs them, and everything else works without them.

## Accessibility <!-- accessibility -->

- **Reduce Motion.** The windows and the clock stop animating: figures change at once and the colon stays lit.
- **Reduce Transparency.** Cadran’s glass and the clock’s glass ink turn solid.
- **Increase Contrast.** Clock text turns fully opaque, its shadows strengthen, and so do the editor’s outlines.
- **VoiceOver.** Cards, bars, rows and controls carry labels, values and actions, and changes of state are announced.
- **Move & Resize with VoiceOver.** VoiceOver reads the clock’s size and offers Move and size actions. `Pro`
- **Full keyboard focus.** A clear focus ring follows the keyboard in the gallery, bars, rows and sheets. Tab moves between the editor’s bar and its bubble.
